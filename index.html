Do you come to site?

Yes. Armourtech offers mobile welding and repair work across Greater Sydney and surrounding areas, subject to the job requirements and travel arrangements.

Can you repair excavator buckets or heavy equipment?

Bucket repairs, wear plate packages, hardfacing, attachments and mobile plant steelwork are core services. Describe the equipment and damage so the scope can be assessed.

How much does a welding job cost?

Hourly rates start from $70. Materials, site travel, consumables, equipment needs and any special requirements are discussed and quoted separately where relevant.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Yes. Use the quote form to attach up to three JPG or PNG photos, with a 10 MB combined limit, or email photos directly with your contact details and job location.

Do you take smaller welding jobs?

Yes. Armourtech welcomes general repairs and small fabrication jobs as well as substantial heavy-industry work. Share what you need and Chris can advise on suitability.
